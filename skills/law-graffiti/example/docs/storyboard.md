# Storyboard: AiXenix — «Граффити» (ad)

## Logline

A tired wall of routine gets crossed out by a crew of cans that paint by themselves: «РУТИНУ — РОБОТАМ» → AiXenix · Бизнес на автопилоте · aixenix.com · a paper plane flies off to t.me/aixenix.

## Law of the method

Everything on screen is put on the wall by the crew — spray cans (no hands: the robots), a roller on a pole, stencil cards. Every word is sprayed, rolled or stencilled; drips run by gravity; cards peel off; the plane is a reverse stencil that peels off and flies.

## Numbers

- 90 bpm → beat 0.6667 s = 20 frames; bar 2.6667 s = 80 frames; 9 bars = 24.0 s = 720 frames.
- `b(bar, beat)` = (bar·4 + beat)·20/30 s (0-based). Bar 9 (21.333–24.0, frames 640–719) is a pixel-exact loop.
